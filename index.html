Early game progression

  • Progress the story to earn EXP and level up your character.
  • Jinis can’t be levelled beyond a cap that depends on your player level.
  • If you catch a jini much higher level than you, it will refuse to obey in combat and may flee.
  • Seasonal quests unlock at level 30, so get there soon.

Buddy pods

How Coco fruit pods work

There are two kinds of Moonfall encounter:

  • Moonfall star: a meteor appears and turns into one jini you can encounter.
  • Moonfall meteor shower: several meteors appear and turn into 10 jinis you can encounter.

When you catch a jini with a Coco pod, one of these may trigger (not on every catch). A hidden pity system also guarantees a chromatic jini encounter eventually, and this helps you reach that pity faster.

Jini stats guide

  • Open a jini’s menu and you will see a lot of information, like in the screenshot below.
  • Each jini has a few base stats.
  • Each jini has a specific talent, ranging from Average to Amazing.

Talent

Talent boosts base stats. Each boosted stat gets a bonus of 7–10. The search icon beside the stats graph shows which stats are boosted.

1Average talent: one random stat
2Decent talent: two stats
3Great talent: three stats
3+Amazing talent: three stats, aligned

So what separates Great from Amazing? To see that, you need to know about personality.

Personality

  • Each jini, even within the same species, has its own personality, like calm or reckless.
  • Each personality boosts one stat and lowers another, both by 10%. Example: reckless raises speed and lowers physical defense.
  • Amazing talent ensures that one of its three boosted stats matches the stat boosted by the personality.
  • In game items let you change the personality, change which 3 stats get boosts, and raise a boosted value from 7 up to 10.

Changing stats

Golden mirror

Boosted stat
  • Changes the boosted stat. Example: HP +10% can become P.ATK +10%.
  • Extremely rare. F2P players may only get one per season or so.
  • Can’t be crafted. Event exclusive.

Broken mirror

Nerfed stat
  • Changes the nerfed stat. Example: Speed −10% can become M.ATK −10%.
  • Moderately easy to get.
  • Crafting needs wishing stars.
  • In game description: clears negative effects tied to a jini’s personality.

Golden key

Which 3 stats
  • Lets you change which stats get the bonus.
  • Crafting needs wishing stars.

Silver key

Bonus value
  • Increases the bonus value, up to a maximum of 10. It can’t go beyond 10.
  • Can be crafted.
  • Tip for the nerfed stat: if your jini is a physical attacker, it’s better to nerf magic attack, since you won’t use that attack anyway. Defense, HP and speed always help.
  • Golden mirrors are so rare that it matters to start with an amazing talent and a personality that boosts the right stat.
  • Don’t worry too much about getting it perfect. You can refund your materials if you find the right jini later. Keys and mirrors are not refunded.

Which stats to build

  • Every jini has base stats, and they aren’t spread evenly. Some have more P.ATK than M.ATK. Others have more HP, defense or speed.
  • If a jini’s base P.ATK is higher than its M.ATK, then P.ATK matters most for it. Put your personality and boosted stat there for the most benefit. The same goes for M.ATK the other way round.
  • If P.ATK and M.ATK are equal, you can choose either.
  • We usually want our jinis to attack first, so speed is a good stat to focus on too, though not every time.
  • A jini built for pure damage can usually make use of speed to attack first.
  • Some jinis have counter skills that can counter opponents even if you are slower. This depends on the skill, so read the skill descriptions, which say it clearly.

You may not guess right every time, but this is a start.

Jini types

Apart from basic variants there are four types: chromatic, prismatic, corrupted and exotic. Chromatic and prismatic are purely cosmetic. Corrupted and exotic get extra combat skills.

Chromatic and prismatic can stack, so you can have a chromatic prismatic jini.

Chromatic

Cosmetic
  • A recolour of the base variant, like shinies in Pokémon.
  • Purely cosmetic.
  • No bonus if you release a chromatic jini.
  • Seasonal jinis get seasonal chroma variants that expire when the season ends. After that you can only get them if they return, or by luck from eggs.

Prismatic

Cosmetic
  • Makes certain parts of the jini shiny, with gradient colour and particle effects.
  • It has two gradient colours, and they are random.
  • Black and white is generally considered very rare.
  • Releasing a prismatic jini gives 200 prism pod fragments. You need 1,600 to craft one prism pod.

Corrupted

Combat skills
  • Evil purplish colour on certain parts of the body, with the corrupted bloodline.
  • Comes with 4 completely random, fixed skills. You can’t remove them or teach new skills until you cleanse it with a cleansing agent.
  • Once cleansed, it becomes exotic, learns one random move (RNG) and keeps the 4 original moves. Neither the RNG move nor the 4 original skills can be used in PvP.
Purifying elixir
The cleansing agent used to cleanse corrupted jinis.

Exotic

Combat skills
  • A cleansed jini gets an exotic bloodline.
  • After cleansing it learns skills normally, and those skills are allowed in PvP.
  • You can’t change an exotic bloodline jini into another bloodline.
  • An exotic bloodline can also be forced with an elixir, or found in the overworld.

Chromatic jini farming

  • Start after level 40. Farming costs a lot of Roco coins and can slow your other progress if you do it too early.
  • Each season has a pool of jinis whose chromatic forms are made available. You can only farm those chromatics during that season. The others can’t be farmed unless they join the pool some time later.
  • Chromas have a pity system, prismatics don’t. So use a prism pod when you encounter a chromatic jini.

How pity works

  • Each system triggers pity differently. For example, gathering star points (the ones that give wishing stars) triggers a seasonal encounter.
  • Each seasonal encounter equals 1 pity. Reaching 80 pity guarantees a chromatic.
  • Near the end of the pity, seasonal encounters come more often, so you gain more pity for fewer catches.
  • You can get a chromatic before 80 pity. Each time you get one, the pity resets.
  • S1 has corruption as a pity system where each time you defeat and capture a corrupted jini, it adds one pity.

Three kinds of pity

Species pity

  • Keep catching only one species that has a chroma. Even if another species’ chromatic shows up, your pity doesn’t reset.
  • When the pity is hit, you are guaranteed that species’ chromatic.
  • Set it up by placing fruits in owl statues, or during an event.
  • Seasonal jinis’ chromas can’t be farmed this way, because their fruits aren’t made available.

Element pity

  • Catch jinis of the same element.
  • Works for seasonal jinis.
  • If several jinis of that element have chromatic versions that season, you can’t guarantee which one you get.
  • Species and element pity don’t interfere with each other.

Mix pity

  • If you catch two different jinis of two different classes, the system detects it and tries to trigger this pity.
  • The chroma you get is random.

What it can cost

A chroma can take up to roughly 800 catches. At 3 pods per catch, that’s about 2,400 advanced pods.

Owl statues

Jini fruit
Blue star
  1. Find owl statues across the map and interact with them.
  2. Activate each one by submitting blue stars found around the map. Each statue needs a different amount.
  3. You receive jini fruits as you play. Place a fruit in a statue and it attracts the jinis linked to that fruit in the area, for good, until you remove the fruit.
  4. For battle pass jinis, join a friend who has the pass and ask them to place the jini fruit in a statue. You can then catch them.
